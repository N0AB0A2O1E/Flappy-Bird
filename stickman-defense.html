How to Play

  • Drag units from the sidebar to deploy on the left side
  • Manage gold wisely - earn 55 gold/second
  • Upgrade weapons to boost unit damage
  • Defend your core tower from enemy waves
  • Attack enemy towers to push back and win

💡 Tips

Mix ranged and melee units for balance

Upgrade weapons between waves for better damage

Watch your gold - don't overspend early
